## Project Overview

This project analyses 14 days of sales data and the current inventory of Wanjiku's fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. The aim is to understand the stall's sales performance, identify weekly and daily sales patterns, compare weekday and weekend performance, and determine which products need restocking. The analysis also calculates gross margins to help Wanjiku make more informed inventory and business decisions. The project uses core Python concepts without relying on pandas or NumPy.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Positive weekly sales growth:** Total sales increased from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, representing growth of 10.66%.

2. **Stronger weekend performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, compared with weekday average sales of KES 2,685. Weekend sales were therefore 82.50% higher.

3. **Inventory restocking required:** Tomatoes had 18 kg against a reorder level of 25 kg, while Sukuma had 9 kg against a reorder level of 15 kg. Both products need restocking.
